# Jal Sense India — Data Source Availability Matrix
*Last Audited: November 2026 | Pilot Region: Nagpur, Maharashtra*

| Source Name | Category | Access Method | Geographic Coverage | Finest Granularity | Observation Period | Update Frequency | Status | Limitations & Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Central Ground Water Board (CGWB) / India-WRIS** | Groundwater | Portal CSV/XLSX download; Portal extraction | All India / Nagpur District (9,892 km²) | Observation Well Station (Lat/Long) | 1969–2025 (Seasonal) | 4x / year (Jan, May, Aug, Nov) | **Implemented & Tested** | Station-level point data; taluka aggregates available. Urban city center monitoring points are sparse. [Link](https://indiawris.gov.in/wris/#/groundWater) |
| **Groundwater Surveys and Development Agency (GSDA)** | Groundwater | MAHABHUJALVEDH portal; Seasonal Bulletins | Maharashtra State / Nagpur District | Taluka / 13 Talukas | Periodic (5-yr baselines) | Seasonal assessments | **Configured (Manual)** | Public API not published; periodic state bulletins parsed manually. [Link](https://mrsac.gov.in) |
| **Nagpur Municipal Corp (NMC) & Orange City Water (OCW)** | Tap Water Supply | Utility press releases; Environmental Status Reports | Nagpur City (Gorewada & Kanhan commands) | City-wide narrative | Historical periodic summaries | Daily claimed (~300 samples/day) | **Blocked (No Open Data)** | No open, machine-readable lab dataset published for public access at ward level. |
| **Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS IS 10500:2012)** | Drinking Standards | Documented Specification | National | Standard parameter benchmarks | Current specification | Periodic revision | **Implemented & Tested** | Standard benchmark limits (Acceptable vs Permissible in absence of alternate source). |
| **National Centre for Disease Control (IDSP / NCDC)** | Public Health Alerts | Weekly Outbreak Surveillance PDFs | National / District Level | District level (with village notes) | 2018–2026 weekly series | Weekly | **Implemented & Tested** | Captures reported outbreak clusters only. Absence of report is not proof of absence of disease. [Link](https://ncdc.mohfw.gov.in) |
| **Jal Jeevan Mission (WQMIS)** | Rural Drinking Water | Public Web Dashboard | Rural Habitations / Gram Panchayats | Village / Source | 2021–2026 | Continuous | **Configured** | Applies exclusively to rural talukas outside NMC municipal boundaries. |
